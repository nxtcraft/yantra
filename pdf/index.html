🔒 Why privacy matters for PDF tools

Most online PDF tools upload your file to their server for processing. This is a serious risk for documents like salary slips, Aadhaar cards, PAN cards, bank statements, and legal agreements. Yantra PDF tools process everything in your browser using JavaScript — your document bytes never travel to any server. Close the tab and the data is gone.
