Pomodoro Focus Timer
The Pomodoro Technique splits work into 25-minute focused sprints with 5-minute breaks. After 4 pomodoros, take a 15-minute long break. This tool tracks your tasks, counts completed pomodoros per task, plays a notification sound at each interval, and stores session history locally in your browser. Works offline as a PWA — install it on your phone for desktop/mobile use.
